# Privacy Policy

NeetCode vs LeetCode Checker runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own, and it does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

## What it reads

When you click **Find Missing Problems**, the extension:

- Reads your completed problems from NeetCode. To do that it uses the NeetCode login already saved in your browser, and sends it only to NeetCode's own API (neetcode.io). If that login has expired, it refreshes it through Google's Firebase sign-in service (securetoken.googleapis.com), which NeetCode uses for accounts.
- Reads your solved problems from LeetCode (leetcode.com) using your existing LeetCode login.
- Compares the two lists and highlights the differences on the NeetCode page.

## What it does not do

- It does not send your data to the developer or to any third party.
- It does not save anything: no storage, cookies, analytics or tracking. Results only exist while the popup is open.
- It only runs when you click the button while on neetcode.io.
